Lighting states

Informational only. Lighting hardware is not controlled from this GUI; the highlighted state is the one implied by this session.

  • BootFront and rear: blue, slow pulse
  • ReadyFront: white 50%. Rear: white 20%
  • ManualFront: white 100%. Rear: red 30%
  • AutonomousFront: white 70%, green status. Rear: green 60%
  • Return HomeFront: white 70%. Rear: cyan, slow pulse
  • Obstacle WarningYellow, blinking at 2 Hz
  • Low BatteryOrange at 50%
  • Critical BatteryRed, blinking at 2 Hz
  • ChargingBlue, slow pulse
  • WiFi LostMagenta, blinking at 1 Hz
  • Sensor FaultAlternating red and yellow at 1 Hz
  • Emergency StopSolid red at 100%
